Who We Are

About 9771.help

9771.help is an independent informational website helping Pakistani citizens understand and use the PM Fuel Relief Scheme 2026 — the Rs. 100/litre petrol subsidy accessed via SMS shortcode 9771.

Last updated: October 17, 2026 · 9771.help editorial team

Our Mission

When the government launched the 9771 fuel relief scheme in September 2026, millions of citizens needed one thing: clear, correct instructions. Official announcements were scattered across press releases and TV tickers, while social media filled with wrong SMS formats and scams. 9771.help exists to fix that — one accurate, plain-language guide.

What We Do

  • Verify before publishing: every format, quota, and rule on this site is cross-checked against government announcements and reputable news reporting before it goes live.
  • Update continuously: fuel prices refresh daily via our automated system; scheme rules are revised whenever the government announces changes (see our live prices).
  • Explain in two languages: full guides in English and اردو, because the scheme serves everyone.
  • Never ask for your data: our SMS tools run entirely in your browser — nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere.

Editorial Independence

9771.help is not affiliated with, operated by, or endorsed by the Government of Pakistan, OGRA, NADRA, or any provincial excise department. Registration happens exclusively through the official SMS shortcode 9771 and government channels. We are a citizen-built guide, and we correct our mistakes publicly via dated updates.

How We Fund This Site

9771.help is supported by advertising (Google AdSense and select ad partners). Ads never influence our editorial content, and we clearly label all sponsored placements.
