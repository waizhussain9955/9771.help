How the 9771 Subsidy Changes Your Price

Registered beneficiaries of the 9771 Fuel Relief Scheme don't pay the market rate. Instead, you receive digital fuel tokens via SMS that carry a flat Rs. 100 per litre discount:

  • Motorcycles, rickshaws & Qingqis: weekly Rs. 500 tokens (5 litres each), up to 20 litres/month → save up to Rs. 2,000/month.
  • Cars up to 800cc: Rs. 1,000 token every 10 days (10 litres), up to 30 litres/month → save up to Rs. 3,000/month.

At the pump you present the token code (received by sending TOK to 9771); the discount applies to the token's litres and the rest is billed at the regular rate above.

Why Prices Change Daily

Pakistan moved from a fortnightly to a daily fuel pricing mechanism in July 2026. OGRA now notifies revised ex-depot prices every day based on international oil prices, the rupee exchange rate, and the petroleum levy. That's why checking the current rate — right here — before refuelling matters more than ever.

Petrol Price History (October 2026)

On 17 October 2026, OGRA notified petrol at Rs. 386.30 and high-speed diesel at Rs. 396.96 per litre. With the 9771 subsidy, eligible citizens paid an effective Rs. 286.30 per litre on their quota fuel.

Tip: fuel prices are uniform nationwide at the notified rate — if a pump charges more, file a complaint.
